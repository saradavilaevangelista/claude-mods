# Brain Dump

When you send a long message (150 words or more), Brain Dump holds it and sorts it into **Goal**, **Facts** and **Asks**. Where something could go two ways, it asks you up to three quick questions and adds your answers as **Decisions**. Then it sends the clean version in place of the original.

If the message has timestamps (like 09:15 or 2026-11-06), it skips the questions and turns your notes into a numbered list, and Claude ends its reply with that list ticked off (✅ / ⬜).

If sorting fails, your message goes exactly as you wrote it.
